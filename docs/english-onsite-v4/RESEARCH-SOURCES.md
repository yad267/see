# English On Site — Research Sources (Learning Core)

Curated evidence base for the tutor–learner dialogue core. Prefer meta-analyses and 2020–2026 primary studies; classic foundations kept where still standard in SLA.

## A. Interaction & speaking (dialogue systems)

| Source | Year | What we take |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Hou & Min. *Dialogue-based CALL for L2 speaking: three-level meta-analysis.* ReCALL / Cambridge. | 2025 | Moderate overall effect on speaking (**g ≈ 0.61**). System type, meaning constraint, and modality matter. CF must not kill willingness to communicate. |
| Liu et al. AI-based interactive scaffolding (AIIS) in IDLE speaking. *Interactive Learning Environments.* | 2025 | Gradual AI assistance beats fixed scaffolding for vocabulary, goal-setting, self-evaluation, motivation across sessions. |
| Sok et al. ChatGPT oral / summarization tasks. *TESOL Quarterly.* | 2025 | Structured AI oral tasks can lift speaking performance when prompts constrain the task. |
| Sim. GenAI as roleplay interlocutor for L2 pragmatics. *TESOL Quarterly.* | 2025 | AI roleplay helps short-term pragmatics; peer talk still stronger for longer retention → keep human/peer goals later; AI for high-frequency practice. |
| Cha et al. CHOP — ChatGPT for EFL oral presentation. arXiv:2407.07393. | 2024 | Personalized oral feedback works when UI separates **task talk** from **feedback**. |

## B. Corrective feedback (how the tutor repairs errors)

| Source | Year | What we take |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Li. Timing of CF in L2 learning. *Language Teaching* (Cambridge). | 2025 state-of-art | Immediate CF during the task generally beats post-task delay (Interaction Hypothesis / SCT). Caveats: prior explicit instruction, salience, vocab vs grammar. |
| Brown, Liu & Norouzian. Written CF Bayesian meta-analysis. *Language Teaching Research.* | 2023/26 | Durable moderate accuracy gains; direct / indirect / metalinguistic roughly similar at meta level → pick by **feature + proficiency**, not dogma. |
| Schenck. Optimize CF by grammatical feature (Korean L1 EFL). *Asian-Pacific Journal of Second and Foreign Language Education.* | 2020 | Recasts for L1-similar/simple forms; **explicit / metalinguistic** for complex semantics (articles, conditionals). |
| Aljaafreh & Lantolf. Negative feedback as regulation in the ZPD. *Modern Language Journal.* | 1994 (foundation) | **Graduated** assistance: implicit → explicit only as needed; withdraw help as learner self-regulates. |
| Nassaji. Graduated vs explicit oral CF on writing conferences. *System.* | 2013 | Graduated CF → more self-correction; explicit CF faster but less self-repair. |

## C. Memory: spacing & retrieval

| Source | Year | What we take |
|--------|------|----------------|
| Kim & Webb (thesis / *Language Learning* meta). Spaced practice in L2. | 2022+ | Spacing: medium–large effect; longer gaps win on **delayed** tests. |
| PNAS. Variable retrieval cues in foreign vocabulary. | 2024 | Variable contexts + spaced retrieval beat constant cues; learners **underestimate** variable practice → tutor must push variety. |
| SSLA. Retrieval schedules for L2 collocations. | 2024 | Spacing especially helps **implicit / automatized** knowledge on delayed tests. |
| *Modern Language Journal.* Spaced fill-in-the-blank vs flashcards. | 2023+ | Spacing helps productive activities, not only flashcards. |

## D. Classic SLA pillars still in the core

| Idea | Anchor | Product rule |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Comprehensible input (i+1) | Krashen (use carefully; pair with interaction) | Tutor speech ≤ ~one notch above learner CEFR; paraphrase before translating. |
| Interaction Hypothesis | Long | Meaning first; negotiate form inside a real task. |
| Output / pushed output | Swain | Force short production every turn; don’t monologue. |
| Noticing | Schmidt | One clear focus form per repair cycle. |
| Skill acquisition | DeKeyser | Declarative → procedural practice → automaticity via repetition in context. |
| Affective filter / WTC | Krashen; MacIntyre | Soften face-threat; praise attempt before form; RU only as last scaffold. |
